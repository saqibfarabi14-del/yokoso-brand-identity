01 — Brand Essence

ようこそ

“Yōkoso” means welcome —
and welcome is the whole brand.

From its second-floor dining room above Al-Madani Tower, YOKOSO has become one of Sylhet's most loved tables — a 4.3★ rated pan-Asian restaurant where Japanese, Thai, Chinese and Korean flavours meet Bangladeshi hospitality.

The identity translates that spirit visually: the brush-drawn circle (an ensō of arrival), lantern-petal geometry, and a warm red that reads across a busy street.

WELCOMING

Every guest is received like an honoured arrival.

PAN-ASIAN

Many Asian kitchens, one confident table.

REFINED

Premium without pretension — Sylhet's special-occasion room.

Tone of voice: warm, assured, hospitable. We say “welcome”, not “luxury”.
Signature sign-off: ようこそ、YOKOSOへ。

02 — Logo System

The mark of arrival

The brush-drawn ring is an ensō — a circle of welcome — opened into lantern petals at its heart. It is never redrawn, recoloured outside the palette, or set over busy photography.

Clear space = height of the central petal on all sides. Minimum width: 24 mm print / 90 px digital.

  • ✕  Don't stretch, rotate or re-crop the brush ring
  • ✕  Don't place the red mark on red or photo backgrounds
  • ✕  Don't add shadows, outlines or gradients
  • ✓  Do pair the mark with generous empty space
  • ✓  Do use the mono white mark on Yokoso Red

05 — Graphic Language

Petals from the ensō

Two assets carry the system: the brush ring (used whole, never rebuilt) and the lantern petal drawn from its centre. Petals repeat into borders, menu dividers and packaging patterns — always one colour, always flat.
